# AFTERFALL — Game Overview

## Project status
AFTERFALL is in the concept and pre-production stage. This document describes planned direction, not implemented features.

## High concept
A 3D open-world survival game set on Earth one year after a global nuclear catastrophe. The long-term world-size ambition is approximately 200 × 200 km; this is an aspiration to validate through prototypes and performance testing, not a confirmed technical target.

## Core pillars
- **Explore:** travel through abandoned settlements, cities, wilderness, and underground locations.
- **Survive:** manage essential needs and respond to environmental threats.
- **Scavenge:** search locations for useful supplies and materials.
- **Craft:** turn collected resources into tools and equipment.
- **Build:** establish and improve shelters or bases.
- **Interact:** encounter NPCs, groups, and settlements.
- **Travel:** eventually use suitable forms of transport.

## World concepts
- Abandoned cities and smaller settlements
- Bunkers and underground facilities
- Remote wilderness and resource locations
- NPC communities and points of interest

## Technology direction
- Engine: Unreal Engine 5
- Gameplay code: C++ only; no Blueprint gameplay implementation
- Large-world technology, including World Partition and streaming, must be evaluated in prototypes

## Not decided yet
- Single-player or multiplayer scope
- Target platforms
- Final world scale and streaming strategy
- Detailed setting, factions, and progression systems

## Development principle
Build a small, playable prototype first. Validate the core survival loop and performance before expanding the world or content scope.
